Our Story

Born from a belief that movement changes everything.

She Soars was founded by Emmanuella Animashaun, a fitness enthusiast and Corporate Associate at one of the UK's leading health and wellness companies.

After years of watching women pour themselves into careers, businesses, and everyone else's wellbeing, Emmanuella saw a gap. Not another fitness class. A community. A space where women could move, breathe, laugh, and belong.

Every She Soars experience is designed with intention. You will not just work out. You will walk away feeling seen, energised, and more like yourself than you have in a long time.

Emmanuella Animashaun, Founder

What We Do

Three pillars. One community.

I
Move

Movement that honours the body and quiets the mind. Walk clubs, pilates, and more. Not for aesthetics, but for how it makes you feel.

II
Connect

The friendships you did not know you needed. We create genuine spaces for women to meet, share, and support one another.

III
Soar

Grow into the fullest version of yourself. With community around you, the ceiling disappears. This is where you realise your right to soar.

We are kicking off She Soars with something a little different. Padel tennis, the fastest growing sport in the world, brings all the energy, laughter, and connection we are about. No experience needed. Just show up ready to move and meet your people.
